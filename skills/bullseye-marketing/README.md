# Bullseye Marketing

**Find three marketing channel bets worth testing.**

Marketing & GTM · Version 0.1.0 · Experimental · Reviewed 3 November 2026

[Library](../../README.md) · [Instructions](SKILL.md) · [Example session](examples/first-session.md)

## When to use it

Use this when you are spreading effort across channels, choosing where to find your first customers, or reassessing an acquisition approach. It is designed for founders and small teams who need a practical testing plan.

## Bring

- A product description, target audience and pricing or offer.
- A measurable goal, deadline, budget and available team hours.
- Product/GTM documents or source links, plus any existing funnel and channel results.

A written brief is enough to begin. If the product is new, say there is no historical data. The assistant should distinguish assumptions from observations rather than invent missing metrics.

## Start

```text
Use $bullseye-marketing for my product. Interview me first and ask for my product and GTM sources before recommending channels.
```

The assistant asks 10 tailored questions and waits for your answers. Share files or source pointers in the chat. Source access depends on the assistant's available connections; use an accessible export if needed.

## Receive

| Stage | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Ten interview answers and a source-backed product/GTM brief |
| Outer Ring | One idea and rationale for each of 19 channels |
| Middle Ring | Three candidates, evidence of audience presence, reachable-audience estimates and CAC scenarios |
| Experiments | A 14-day daily plan per candidate with workload, cash caps and decision criteria |
| Review | A comparison of actual results; a winning channel only if the evidence supports one |

Plans may run sequentially if three simultaneous tests would exceed your capacity. Two weeks may only reveal early signals for SEO, partnerships or enterprise sales. Customer presence does not prove buying intent, and leads do not prove paid acquisition.

## Requirements and boundaries

An assistant that can follow multi-turn instructions and read supplied materials. Browsing improves current channel research; without it, claims must remain provisional. There are no scripts to execute, mandatory paid APIs or required MCP servers in this skill.

The package was structurally validated in Codex. It has not been independently field-tested across products or assistants. It does not guarantee customers or authorize campaign execution.
