# Customized prompt

Populate this after the interview and evidence review. The original instruction to ask 10 questions has already been fulfilled; do not restart the interview when executing this prompt.

```text
My product in one sentence: [product, audience and outcome]
My target audience: [segment, buyer/user, geography, language]
My goal: [metric, baseline, target and deadline]
My current traction: [dated observed usage, paid customers, revenue and retention]
My budget: [currency, monthly cash budget and experiment cash cap]
Team size: [roles, skills and available hours per week]

Buying problem and trigger: [pain, urgency and alternatives]
Offer and economics: [pricing, margin, sales cycle and CAC/payback constraint]
Previous channel results: [activity, spend, funnel counts and periods]
Existing advantages: [owned audience, relationships, assets and distribution]
Constraints: [product readiness, market access and operational limits]
Evidence reviewed: [source links/files, dates and what each establishes]
Unknowns and assumptions: [explicit gaps, uncertainty and sensitivity]

Task 1 — Discovery completed:
Use my 10 interview answers and reviewed evidence above. Resolve only material
remaining contradictions; do not repeat answered questions or invent data.

Task 2 — Build the Bullseye:
Give one specific idea and a one-line rationale for each of the 19 channels.
Select the three strongest test candidates. For each provide estimated CAC
with assumptions, total addressable channel audience versus realistically
reachable audience, evidence of customer presence, risks and confidence.
Keep the Inner Ring empty until actual test results justify a winner.

Task 3 — Build experiments:
Give each of the three candidates a 14-day test with daily actions, cash and
time caps, metrics, attribution, success/failure/inconclusive criteria and
a review decision. Fit the combined schedule to my resources. Distinguish
paid customer outcomes from early signals and label long-cycle uncertainty.
```
